# GryphCal Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 24, 2026_

GryphCal is a Chrome extension that puts your University of Guelph CourseLink
assignments and quizzes on one calendar. This policy explains what data the
extension uses and what happens to it.

**Short version:** everything stays in your browser. GryphCal has no servers,
no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your data is never sent to the developer
or to anyone else.

## What GryphCal reads

When you have CourseLink (`courselink.uoguelph.ca`) open and are signed in,
GryphCal uses your existing CourseLink session to read:

- **Your name and CourseLink username.** Used only to show a welcome card.
- **Your course list.** Course codes, names, terms and dates, so you can pick
  which courses to show.
- **Assignments and quizzes** in the courses you pick: titles and due dates.
- **Your submission status** for assignments: whether and when you submitted.
- **Your grades** for those items, if your instructor has released them.

GryphCal does **not** read or store your password, cookies, messages, files you
submitted, course content, or anything from any site other than CourseLink. It
does not track your browsing.

## How it is used

The data above is used only to show your calendar, deadlines, submission status
and grades inside the extension. Nothing else.

## Where it is stored

Everything is saved in your browser using Chrome's local extension storage
(`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device. Your light/dark theme choice is
also saved locally. None of it is synced to your Google account or uploaded
anywhere.

## Sharing

GryphCal does **not** sell, share, transfer or send your data to anyone,
including the developer. The only network requests the extension makes go
directly from your browser to CourseLink, the same way the CourseLink website
itself does.

## Permissions

- **`storage`**: to save your calendar data on your device.
- **Access to `courselink.uoguelph.ca`**: to read your courses, deadlines and
  grades from CourseLink while you are signed in.

## Deleting your data

Removing the extension (`chrome://extensions` → GryphCal → Remove) deletes all
data it has stored. Nothing is kept anywhere else, so there is nothing further
to delete.

## Limited Use

GryphCal's use of information follows the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq),
including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the
extension's single purpose (showing your CourseLink deadlines), is not
transferred to third parties, is not used for advertising, and is not used to
determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. No human reads your data.

## Affiliation

GryphCal is an independent student project. It is not made, endorsed or
supported by the University of Guelph or D2L.
